# German Course Plan

## Purpose

This is the original production plan for a 450 lesson German course from first contact through B2. It defines scope, sequence, outcomes, and evidence expectations. It does not approve linguistic claims or grant redistribution rights. Every lesson must still become a versioned content pack, pass deterministic validation, and receive competent human review before learner release.

The course treats the learner as a person doing meaningful things with language. Grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, reading, listening, writing, speaking, online interaction, and mediation are integrated into practical situations. Lesson completion records exposure. Deterministic assessed evidence controls progression and review.

## Research basis

1. The [Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4) provides the level framework and expands the original model with mediation, online interaction, phonology, and plurilingual competence.
2. The [Council of Europe descriptor collection](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors) provides structured ability descriptors for curriculum and assessment alignment.
3. [Goethe Institut exam training](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/prf/ueb.html) confirms that listening, reading, writing, and speaking need explicit preparation at every assessed level.
4. The [Goethe Institut German examination overview](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/prf.html) provides level appropriate assessment context and official vocabulary resources through B1.
5. The [BAMF integration course framework](https://www.bamf.de/SharedDocs/Anlagen/DE/Integration/Integrationskurse/Kurstraeger/KonzepteLeitfaeden/rahmencurriculum-integrationskurs.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=8) grounds the domain sequence in everyday, social, civic, educational, and working life.
6. The [VHS Lernportal course overview](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/kursangebot.php) demonstrates a practical scene led structure with systematic vocabulary and grammar, recurring stories, varied tasks, and all four primary skills.
7. The [VHS Lernportal teaching resources](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/unterrichtsmaterial.php) provide official lesson grouped vocabulary lists and an A1 through B1 audiovisual progression that can be used for independent coverage checks.
8. The [IDS grammis systematic grammar](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik) is the primary reference for German syntax, semantics, valency, and communicative function.
9. The [2024 official German spelling rules](https://www.rechtschreibrat.com/DOX/RfdR_Amtliches-Regelwerk_2024.pdf) are the authority for spelling and punctuation claims.

These sources define standards and coverage. They are not permission to copy exercises, dialogues, recordings, images, or proprietary lesson sequences.

## Course shape

The plan contains 45 units with 10 lessons each.

1. A1 contains Units 1 through 9 and Lessons 001 through 090.
2. A2 contains Units 10 through 19 and Lessons 091 through 190.
3. B1 contains Units 20 through 31 and Lessons 191 through 310.
4. B2 contains Units 32 through 45 and Lessons 311 through 450.

Each unit normally includes a scene, focused vocabulary, a form and meaning pattern, listening, guided speaking, reading, writing or online interaction, pronunciation, mediation, and a capstone. Review scheduling is separate and adapts to assessed learner evidence.

## Content production contract

Each lesson becomes a reviewed pack item with the following minimum structure.

1. One learner outcome written as an observable action.
2. Prerequisite concept identifiers and a CEFR approximation.
3. A bounded vocabulary set with lemma, article where relevant, plural where relevant, meaning, register, source notes, and audio script.
4. One form and meaning explanation backed by an identified source.
5. At least two original examples reviewed for naturalness, meaning, region, and register.
6. A listening or reading input with provenance and redistribution status.
7. A speaking, writing, interaction, or mediation activity with deterministic acceptance criteria.
8. A pronunciation target only when the app can evaluate it honestly.
9. Error rules that distinguish meaning blocking errors from developmental variation.
10. Review status, reviewer identity, review date, license status, and content version.

## Animated lesson rendering

The content pack describes meaning and interaction. The app chooses a native visual card pattern.

1. Scene cards reveal a setting, goal, and participant roles in a short entrance sequence.
2. Focus cards animate only the changed word, sound, or clause position.
3. Listening cards reveal meaning after playback and never animate decorative motion during audio.
4. Assembly cards let the learner build a phrase through deterministic choices.
5. Contrast cards move two examples into alignment so the meaningful difference is visible.
6. Scenario cards use short turns with visible progress and an explicit exit.
7. Mediation cards show source information, audience, and the details that must survive.
8. Recap cards summarize what was visited and clearly separate exposure from mastery.
9. Motion lasts only long enough to show change, respects reduced motion, and never blocks input.
10. Decorative generation cannot add a linguistic fact that is absent from the reviewed pack.

## Local model role

The local model may propose example variants, distractors, hints, scene wording, difficulty labels, or a future lesson candidate. It receives bounded identifiers and source claims. Its output is a proposal only.

The deterministic pipeline validates schema, identifiers, length, allowed vocabulary, prerequisite references, answer coverage, state transitions, and provenance fields. A proposed lesson enters an authoring queue. It cannot become available to learners, alter mastery, schedule review, or unlock progression until the required deterministic and human review gates pass.

Learner tracking remains local. Assessed attempts update explicit evidence dimensions. The planner uses those dimensions, prerequisite readiness, due review, recent exposure, and configured variety to suggest an existing approved lesson. Model output may improve phrasing after acceptance, but cannot select the authoritative next state.
