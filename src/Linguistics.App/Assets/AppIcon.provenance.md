## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: macOS desktop application icon, master square raster

Primary request: Create a polished, instantly recognizable icon for a local-first
language learning app called Linguistics, expressed as a tactile paper theatre.

Scene/backdrop: A deep forest green rounded-square app tile floating on a genuinely
transparent canvas.

Subject: A compact warm-cream layered paper proscenium or open stage, with two
overlapping speech-shaped paper cutouts suggesting conversation and language learning.
Include a small amber paper-tab accent as the single accent family.

Style/medium: Premium handcrafted cut-paper collage, subtle recycled paper grain,
gently irregular cut edges, shallow tinted contact shadows, calm editorial design,
not childish.

Composition/framing: Centered, bold silhouette, balanced negative space, readable at
16 px, minimal detail, generous safe margin for macOS masking.

Lighting/mood: Soft diffuse studio light, tactile and welcoming.

Color palette: Deep forest green, warm cream and parchment, muted amber only. No bright
rainbow colors.

Text: none.

Constraints: 1:1 square; actual transparent background outside the rounded-square tile;
no letters, words, flags, faces, books, globes, microphones, graduation caps, pencils,
mascots, gradients, glossy 3D plastic, watermark, or extra decorative objects. Keep the
speech cutouts and paper stage as the only central metaphor.
